# BosskuAI Content Calendar Playbook

## Calendar creation workflow

1. Define audience segment and conversion goal.
2. Pick 4–7 content pillars.
3. Map each post to one intent: educate, prove, compare, handle objection, show process, ask engagement, sell softly.
4. Attach CTA per audience and funnel stage.
5. Add measurable signal per post.
6. Repurpose high-performing topics into video, carousel, thread, email, and landing-page FAQ.

## Quality bar

- Hook is specific, not generic.
- Post explains one idea only.
- CTA matches intent.
- Local tone is natural, not forced.
- No unsupported statistics.
- Calendar includes enough variety to avoid feed fatigue.

## Metrics layer

Every calendar row should include metrics: leading signal, lagging signal, platform metric, CTA metric, and review cadence. Example: replies, saves, clicks, vendor signups, customer leads, booked calls, and conversion rate.
