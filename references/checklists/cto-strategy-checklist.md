# CTO Strategy Checklist

> If the request is general, ambiguous, or touches many files — ask clarifying yes/no questions **before acting**. Use numbered bullets with explicit answer format: e.g. `1-yes/no  2-A/B`.

- Is the business goal, stage, runway, and customer profile stated before any technology recommendation?
- Are the irreversible decisions (data model, tenancy model, core language, cloud account structure, region) named and given the most deliberation?
- Does each option carry a 3-year TCO including operations, exit cost, lock-in, compliance fit, and hiring market?
- Is "build" limited to differentiators, with context bought and open source adopted only where the team can operate it?
- Is the one-page technology strategy current: bets, build vs buy, platforms, non-negotiables, explicit "not this year"?
- Do architecture principles exist that reviewers can cite?
- Is the debt ledger current with monthly interest, cost to fix, owner, and a pay/refinance/tolerate decision?
- Is the risk register current with owners: key-person, vendor lock-in, single points of failure, compliance deadlines?
- Is platform work budgeted at a stated share of capacity in the 12–18 month roadmap?
- Are DORA, SLO, cost per tenant, and security metrics tracked with targets and trends?
- Are baseline security controls in place (SSO/MFA, least privilege, secrets manager, tested backups, logging, scanning, incident plan)?
- Is the compliance path (PDPA data map, SOC 2 / ISO 27001 timing) forecast from the sales pipeline rather than reacted to?
- Does the hiring plan target capability-map gaps with levels and bands written down?
- Is the cloud budget alerted at 50/80/100% and are reserved commitments deferred until steady state?
- Is the diligence data room maintained: architecture, security policies, DR evidence, SBOM/licenses, IP assignment?
- Is the decision written as an ADR with an owner and a review cadence?
