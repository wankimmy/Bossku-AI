# Bosskuai Customer Success Support Checklist

Use this checklist only when the task clearly needs this domain.

- Define support categories, severity, SLA, owner, and escalation path.
- Create onboarding checklist for first value, not just account setup.
- Convert repeated tickets into product fixes, docs, or automation.
- Track churn reason, activation, time-to-first-value, and support burden.
- Do not hide unresolved product problems behind customer success scripts.

## Release Gate

- Confirm what was verified.
- State what remains unverified.
- Add regression test, metric, SOP, or rollback trigger where applicable.
- Save durable memory only for stable decisions, preferences, constraints, or reusable lessons.
