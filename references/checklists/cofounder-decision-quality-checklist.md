# Cofounder Decision Quality Checklist

- [ ] Names current stage and primary objective.
- [ ] Names the main constraint.
- [ ] Separates confirmed evidence from assumptions.
- [ ] Considers at least two plausible options when a decision is requested.
- [ ] Gives one recommendation, not a menu dump.
- [ ] Explains tradeoff and what not to do.
- [ ] Routes to the minimum expert skill set.
- [ ] Includes metric, verification method, or customer proof signal.
- [ ] Includes risk and rollback where execution can break production, security, money, or reputation.
- [ ] Avoids motivational language and generic founder advice.
