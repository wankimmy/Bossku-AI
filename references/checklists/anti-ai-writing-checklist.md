# Anti-AI Writing Checklist

Use this before publishing README copy, landing pages, docs, posts, emails, and UI microcopy.

## Reject

- Generic hype: `unlock`, `elevate`, `seamless`, `revolutionary`, `game-changing`, `cutting-edge`.
- Startup filler: `powerful`, `robust`, `comprehensive`, `effortless` without proof.
- Template framing: `whether you're...`, `not just X, but Y`, `in today's fast-paced world`.
- Fake certainty: claims that sound proven but have no measurement.
- Over-polished rhythm: every paragraph has the same length and structure.
- Decorative em dashes, forced triads, and dramatic contrast lines.

## Prefer

- Specific workflow, file, metric, or example.
- Direct limitation when the product cannot guarantee an outcome.
- Short line first, explanation second.
- Concrete verb over broad adjective.
- Human texture: slightly varied sentence lengths, no forced perfection.

## Final Pass

Ask:

1. Could this text belong to any AI SaaS landing page?
2. Is one claim unsupported?
3. Is one sentence too polished for the audience?
4. Can one specific example replace one vague phrase?
5. Would the intended user actually say this?

If yes, rewrite once.
