Visual rules

Use

  • BosskuAI emerald for active work and successful checks
  • Zinc canvas and strong contrast for readable screen captures
  • Large system sans typography with concise feature titles
  • Slow push-in, proof close-up, then context pull-back

Avoid

  • Fabricated dashboard UI or artificial metrics
  • Small text that cannot be read at video size
  • Generic AI gradients and rapid-cut motion
  • Claims that cannot be proved by a shown screen
